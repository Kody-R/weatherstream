# WeatherStream v0.3.8 — RWN Broadcast Motion & Transitions

v0.3.8 moves the approved RWN visual system from static boards into restrained television-style motion. It is an additive presentation release: data sources, channel logic, Weather Story classification, Event Channel Identity, Studio rundowns, and v0.3.7 branding remain intact.

## Broadcast Motion 2.0

When enabled, WeatherStream resolves transitions by context rather than applying one effect to every channel:

- Normal RWN: signature logo wipe (`clean`), soft crossfade, or panel push
- Severe Weather Center: angular red/orange warning wipe
- Flood & Hydrology Desk: animated waterline wipe
- Winter Weather Desk: staggered ice-shard/pane reveal
- Heat Weather Desk: restrained heat shimmer
- Fire Weather Desk: softened smoke dissolve
- RWN Tropics Watch: clockwise radar-sweep reveal

The old fixed transition selector remains available and is used whenever automatic transitions are disabled.

## Screen-entry motion

After a transition, static boards can use a very short settle animation. Desk context selects from broadcast lift, impact settle, glide, or soft-focus entry styles. The animation changes presentation only; it never changes the timing or meaning of weather data.

## Severe-weather behavior

Qualifying severe-warning takeovers bypass decorative transitions when `emergency_hard_cut` is enabled (default). This makes the warning board appear immediately.

## Performance and compatibility

- Motion is rendered on the existing Visual System 2.0 canvas and scales with the final frame.
- Adaptive performance degradation can still force the existing hard-cut mode.
- Local-on-the-8s forced phases continue to bypass the normal transition timeline so TTS and video stay synchronized.
- Schema advances from 25 to 26; existing v0.3.7 transition settings and custom sequences are preserved.
