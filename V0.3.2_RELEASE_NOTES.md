# WeatherStream v0.3.2 — Forecast Graphics 2.0

v0.3.2 builds directly on the resolution-independent Visual System 2.0 foundation from v0.3.1. The release focuses on forecast storytelling: what changes next, when it changes, how uncomfortable or windy it becomes, and how much rain may accumulate.

## New broadcast graphics

- **Next 24 Hours** — six TV-readable checkpoints across the configured 12–24 hour window, with weather icons, temperature/feels-like, rain chance, 24-hour range, peak rain probability, rainfall total, and peak gust.
- **Humidity & Dew Point** — 12-hour relative-humidity trend plus calculated dew-point values and plain-language comfort messaging such as VERY HUMID, OPPRESSIVE HUMIDITY, or VERY DRY AIR.
- **Wind Outlook** — sustained-wind and gust trends, directional arrows/cardinal directions, and contextual LIGHT WINDS / BREEZY / GUSTY / STRONG WINDS labeling.
- **Rainfall Accumulation** — cumulative 24-hour rainfall curve with individual hourly contribution bars and 6-, 12-, and 24-hour totals.
- **NWS Forecast Brief** — turns the official NWS forecast periods into a broadcast hierarchy with temperature, short forecast, precipitation probability, wind, and concise bullets derived from the official detailed forecast text.

## Smarter rundown behavior

Smart Programming now considers:

- next-12-hour precipitation probability,
- next-24-hour forecast precipitation accumulation,
- next-12-hour peak gust,
- forecast dew-point range,
- thunderstorm guidance,
- and SPC Day 1 risk.

When Smart Story Ordering is enabled, notable rain, wind, humidity, and severe-weather graphics move earlier in the rundown instead of appearing at a fixed position. Quiet-weather graphics can be omitted when their thresholds are not met.

## Data changes

The existing Open-Meteo hourly request now also asks for hourly wind direction and cloud cover. No new third-party service is required for Forecast Graphics 2.0. Dew point and rainfall accumulation are derived locally from already-fetched forecast data.

NWS forecast ingestion now retains forecast precipitation probability, relative humidity when supplied, and dew point when supplied for future presentation work.

## Admin / Studio

- Forecast Graphics 2.0 master toggle.
- 12–24 hour day-ahead horizon.
- Individual toggles for Wind Outlook, Humidity/Dew Point, Rain Accumulation, and the new NWS brief.
- Smart-programming thresholds for wind gusts, humid dew point, and dry dew point.
- Smart Story Ordering toggle.
- Individual durations for every new slide.
- All four new graphics are available in WeatherStream Studio rundown editing and previews.

## Upgrade compatibility

Settings schema advances from 19 to **20**. Existing v0.3.1 sequences are preserved; the new forecast graphics are inserted adjacent to their related forecast slides without replacing the user's existing ordering. All existing regions, channel overrides, branding, Studio schedules, encoder settings, radar, tropical, event-channel, TTS, and Visual System settings are retained.
