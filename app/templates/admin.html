RWN Network Channels

v0.1.8.1 automatically builds one HLS channel per ZIP plus dedicated Radar and Severe Weather channels. All channels are published in /playlist.m3u and /guide.xml.

MULTI-CHANNEL

Realtime Video Performance

v0.1.8.1 uses a lower-cost multi-channel profile and reports whether each HLS producer is actually keeping up with wall-clock time.

PERFORMANCE

Recommended multi-channel defaults: 5 pipe FPS, 3 content FPS, 15 output FPS, superfast, 2000k, 3-second HLS segments, 10-segment window. Higher values increase CPU usage.
