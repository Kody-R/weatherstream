# WeatherStream v0.3.6 — RWN Weather Story Engine

v0.3.6 changes the local channel from a condition-aware slide rotation into an adaptive weather broadcast. The director evaluates the data WeatherStream already has, chooses the strongest current story, and builds a coherent NOW → NEXT → LATER → CONTEXT rundown around that story.

## Story classification

The director can classify:

- Quiet Weather
- Rain Approaching
- Storms Possible
- Severe Weather
- Heat & Humidity
- Cold Weather
- Winter Weather
- Flooding / Heavy Rain
- High Wind
- Poor Air Quality
- Tropical Weather
- Fire Weather / Smoke

Official NWS warning/event signals receive higher priority than model-only forecast signals. A Tornado Warning, for example, outranks ordinary rain or high wind. River-gauge trends are treated as context and do not independently create a flood story; WeatherStream continues to avoid inventing flood-stage determinations.

## Structured broadcast direction

Each story contains four programming blocks:

- **NOW** — the immediate conditions/hazard picture
- **NEXT** — timing, radar, risk, and short-range forecast products
- **LATER** — the longer-range forecast
- **CONTEXT** — history, climate, AQI, hydrology, or other background products

The engine filters that ideal story plan against the products available to the active local channel and the user's enabled feature settings.

## Supporting stories

The strongest story controls the broadcast, but other meaningful signals are retained. For example, if high wind scores slightly above rain, Wind Outlook can lead while Rain Timing and Rain Accumulation remain in the NEXT block. This replaces the older one-dimensional promotion behavior with a more editorial rundown.

## Story stabilization

Ordinary forecast stories can be held for a configurable number of minutes so the channel does not alternate between two stories when a value sits near a threshold. High-impact official stories are not artificially held after their trigger clears.

## The Weather Story graphic

A new `story_brief` slide shows:

- Active RWN story
- Why the story was selected
- Up to three supporting signals
- The lead product in NOW, NEXT, LATER, and CONTEXT

The slide is optional and has its own duration control.

## Context-aware lower third

When enabled, the data ribbon reflects the active story. Rain/flood/storm stories emphasize probability and forecast rainfall; heat emphasizes peak heat; wind emphasizes gusts; air quality emphasizes AQI; cold/winter emphasizes the next low; tropical stories indicate that an official tropical update is active.

Severe-warning ticker takeover still has priority over the normal story ribbon.

## Studio and dashboard

WeatherStream Studio now displays the current automatic classification and its four story blocks. **Load Automatic Story** copies the generated sequence into the Studio editor without publishing it. This makes the auto director inspectable while keeping manual control explicit.

The Broadcast Dashboard now reports the active story, score, signals, held/live state, and adaptive rundown length.

## Overrides and compatibility

- Severe Weather Takeover remains highest priority.
- Published Studio rundowns override automatic story composition.
- Radar, Severe, Tropics Watch, and automatic Event channels keep their specialized sequences; Story Engine primarily directs normal Local channels.
- Disabling Story Engine falls back to the existing daypart + smart-programming behavior.
- Schema 24 upgrades v0.3.5 settings without replacing custom sequences. The optional `story_brief` is inserted after Current Conditions where applicable.

## New settings

`story_engine` adds:

- `enabled`
- `show_story_brief`
- `include_context`
- `context_ribbon`
- `max_slides`
- `hold_minutes`
- `aqi_threshold`
- `heavy_rain_inches`
- `high_wind_gust`

## API

`GET /api/story` returns the active story classification, evidence, metrics, story sections, and ideal sequence for a selected location/channel mode. `/api/status` and `/api/rundown/preview` also expose story information.

## No new external dependency

The v0.3.6 director consumes data already supplied by the v0.3.5 WeatherStream pipeline. No additional API account, key, container, or service is required.
